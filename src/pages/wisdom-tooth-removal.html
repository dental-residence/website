Wisdom teeth

Wisdom Tooth Removal

Wisdom teeth: the last to arrive, and often the first to cause trouble.

If yours are aching, swelling or simply out of room, we'll check them, explain your options, and only take them out if it makes sense.

Need a different tooth out? See tooth removal.

A gentle word of advice

Step away from the string.

There's a far comfier way to say goodbye to a tooth.

The comfy part

Do I need local or general anaesthetic?

Whatever keeps you comfortable. Depending on your needs and how tricky the tooth is, we can use:

  • Local anaesthetic. Numbs the area while you stay awake.
  • Happy gas (nitrous oxide). Takes the edge off nerves.
  • Oral sedation. A prescribed sedative before you come in, so you're relaxed but awake.
  • General anaesthetic. Fully asleep. Let us know before your appointment so we can check it's right for you and arrange it.

Having oral sedation or a general anaesthetic? Bring someone to take you home.

The big question

Do I need to get my wisdom tooth removed?

Not always. Plenty of people live with theirs for years. But a wisdom tooth that keeps getting infected, or doesn't have room to come through properly, can mean ongoing pain and problems for the teeth next to it.

The easiest way to know is to let us take a look.
