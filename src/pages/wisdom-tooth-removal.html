Wisdom Tooth Removal

Wisdom teeth can be annoying, uncomfortable and down right painful! They can also cause over crowding in your mouth, which his can lead to a crooked smile.

While a wisdom tooth extraction is a standard procedure, it is important to get the right information on the best treatment for you. That is why we recommend a free consultation with one of our wisdom teeth specialists.

Do I need local or general anaesthetic?

We offer a range of anaesthetic and sedation options to help make your wisdom tooth removal as comfortable as possible.
​These may include local anaesthetic, oral sedation, happy gas (nitrous oxide), or general anaesthetic, depending on your individual needs and the complexity of your procedure.
If you believe you may require or would prefer your treatment under general anaesthetic, please contact our team before your appointment. This allows us to discuss your options, determine whether general anaesthetic is appropriate, and make the necessary arrangements in advance if required.
Oral sedation involves taking a prescribed sedative before your appointment to help you feel relaxed while remaining awake. Happy gas (nitrous oxide) can also be used to reduce anxiety during treatment.
Please note that if you receive oral sedation or general anaesthetic, you will need someone to accompany you home after your procedure.

Do I need to get my wisdom tooth removed?

Some people put up with the discomfort of painful wisdom teeth for many years before seeking an extraction. The pain can be managed with over the counter pain killers like paracetamol.

However, not removing wisdom teeth can have long term implications. Recurring infections can lead to sore jaws, migraine and even gut problems.

Over crowding of the back wisdom teeth can also cause the front teeth to become crooked. This could require expensive straightening treatments to correct in the future.

Rather than wait, we invite you to come for a free consultation to determine what option is right for you.
