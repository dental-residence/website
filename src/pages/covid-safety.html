COVID Safety Message

Providing dental care to the community is considered an essential reason for leaving home under stay-at-home orders. Therefore, we remain open to service the community.

However we ask that if you are experiencing any flu-like symptoms, you stay home and reschedule your appointment.

Extra COVID Safety Measures

While keeping an exceptional level of hygiene and sterilisation is an intrinsic part of life at our practice, we have implemented special safety measures to ensure the safety of a patients and staff. These include:
  •  Requesting that any patients who are displaying symptoms reschedule their appointments.
  • Reserving the right to refuse treatment to patients who display symptoms.
  • Requiring checkin for all patients, family of patients and contractors that enter the premises.
  • Requiring a infra-red temperature check and temperatures to be recorded.
  • Requiring the use of personal protective equipment (PPE) that help stop the spread of infectious diseases.
