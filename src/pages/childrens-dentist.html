We love our younger patients. With a friendly team and no rush, first visits are about getting comfortable, so children grow up seeing the dentist as somewhere easy to be, not something to fear.

Child Dental Benefits Schedule

Bulk Billing for Children Under 18

If your child is aged 2 to 17, has a valid Medicare card and your family receives certain government payments, their dental treatment may be bulk billed under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS).

  • Check-ups
  • X-rays
  • Cleaning
  • Fissure sealing
  • Fillings
  • Root canals
  • Extractions

Check your child’s eligibility on the Services Australia website, or ask us when you book.

Good questions

Children's Dental FAQs

When should my child first see a dentist?

Around their first birthday, or when their first teeth arrive. Early, easy visits help children feel relaxed in the chair.

How long is a child check-up?

About 30 minutes, including a check of their teeth and gums and a clean.

Is my child covered by the CDBS?

Children aged 2 to 17 may be eligible if they have a valid Medicare card and the family receives certain government payments. Check on the Services Australia website, or ask us when you book.

Who will my child see?

Dr Anna Yip, Dr Mac Dean or Rita Chen, our oral health therapist.
