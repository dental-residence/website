Bulk Billing for Children Under 18
Did you know that if you or your child has received a government payment, then your child's treatment may be eligible to be entirely bulk billed?

If your child is between 2-17 and has a valid medicare care then the government provides up to $1013 for dental treatment within each two-year period under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS).

Treatments covered include:
  • check-ups
  • X-rays
  • cleaning
  • fissure sealing
  • fillings
  • root canals
  • extractions.

To find out more and if you are eligible then go to the Services Australia Website.
