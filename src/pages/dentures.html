Dentures

Partial Dentures

Dentures are an affordable solution whether your missing one tooth or many. Partial dentures start from $800 depending on the complexity of the denture.

A denture is a prosthetic tooth or set of teeth that can be slipped into and out of the mouth to replace missing teeth. They look and act very similar to real teeth.

Full Dentures

If very few healthy teeth remain, it may be necessary to go to a full denture, which involve removing the remaining teeth and providing you with a full set of artificial teeth.

Special Considerations

The disadvantages of dentures is that being a prosthetic they can cause slight discomfort or irritation. They can move while eating, talking etc and they have to be cleaned regularly.

Alternative Solutions

If you are looking for a more permanent solution, then you may like to consider a dental bridge or dental implants. Each of these has their pros and cons but we provide free 30 consultations for dental bridges and implants to help you navigate the various issues.
