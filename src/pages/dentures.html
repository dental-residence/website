Partial & full dentures

Dentures

A full smile again, whether you're missing one tooth or most of them.

Dentures are replacement teeth that slip in and out, and look and work a lot like the real thing. Got most of your own teeth? A partial denture fills just the gaps.

We make new ones, fix old ones, and tell you honestly which option suits you.

Starting fresh

Full Dentures

If only a few healthy teeth are left, a full denture may be the answer: the remaining teeth come out and you get a complete new set.

The honest bit

Special Considerations

  • Comfort. They can rub or feel a little uncomfortable.
  • Movement. They can shift a bit when you eat or talk.
  • Care. They need a regular clean, just like teeth.

Something more permanent?

Alternative Solutions

Prefer something that stays put? A dental bridge or dental implants are fixed options. Each has its pros and cons, and you can talk them through in a free consultation.
