Partial & full dentures · Petersham

Dentures

A full smile again, whether you’re missing one tooth or most of them.

Book Online Call (02) 8384 6667

Dentures are replacement teeth that slip in and out, and look and work a lot like the real thing. Got most of your own teeth? A partial denture fills just the gaps. We make new ones, fix old ones, and tell you honestly which option suits you.

Full Dentures

If only a few healthy teeth are left, a full denture may be the answer: the remaining teeth come out and you get a complete new set.

The honest bit

Special Considerations

  1. ComfortThey can rub or feel a little uncomfortable.
  2. MovementThey can shift a bit when you eat or talk.
  3. CareThey need a regular clean, just like teeth.

Something more permanent?

Alternative Solutions

Prefer something that stays put? A dental bridge or dental implants are fixed options. Each has its pros and cons, and you can talk them through in a free consultation.

Good questions

Dentures FAQs

Partial or full denture?

A partial denture fills the gaps when you still have most of your own teeth. A full denture replaces a whole arch. We’ll tell you honestly which suits you.

Can you fix my old dentures?

Often, yes. Bring them in and we’ll take a look.

How do I look after them?

Clean them every day, just like teeth, and keep up your regular check-ups so we can check the fit.

Are there fixed options?

Yes: a bridge or dental implants. We can talk them through at a free consultation.
