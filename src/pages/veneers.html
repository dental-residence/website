Porcelain Veneers

Veneers are one of the most popular cosmetic treatments to instantly improve the look of your smile. A veneer is a thin custom made porcelain applied directly to the teeth. Porcelain has natural translucency that truly mimics natural tooth structure and gives your smile enhanced aesthetics.

​They can correct a multitude of imperfections, including changing the colour and shape of teeth, making crooked teeth appear straight, correcting gaps and spaces between teeth, and making the arch wider (which creates a fuller smile by giving greater support to the lips and cheeks).
