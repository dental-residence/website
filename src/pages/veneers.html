Porcelain Veneers

A thin layer of porcelain. A whole new look.

Book Online Call (02) 8384 6667

Veneers are custom-made porcelain shells bonded to the front of your teeth. Porcelain has a natural translucency, so they look like teeth, not like dental work. Curious? Start with a free consultation and we’ll tell you honestly whether they’re right for you.

The makeover list

What can veneers fix?

  1. ColourBrighten or even out the shade of your teeth.
  2. ShapeReshape teeth that are uneven or worn.
  3. StraightnessMake slightly crooked teeth look straight.
  4. GapsClose small gaps and spaces between teeth.

The honest answer

Are veneers right for me?

Not always, and they’re not the only route to a brighter smile. Teeth whitening or Invisalign® might do the job instead.

We’ll talk through all of it at your free consultation.
