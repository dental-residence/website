Porcelain Veneers

A thin layer of porcelain. A whole new look.

Veneers are custom-made porcelain shells bonded to the front of your teeth. Porcelain has a natural translucency, so they look like teeth, not like dental work.

Curious? Start with a free consultation and we'll tell you honestly whether they're right for you.

The makeover list

What can veneers fix?

  • Colour. Brighten or even out the shade of your teeth.
  • Shape. Reshape teeth that are uneven or worn.
  • Straightness. Make slightly crooked teeth look straight.
  • Gaps. Close small gaps and spaces between teeth.

The honest answer

Are veneers right for me?

Not always, and they're not the only route to a brighter smile. Teeth whitening or Invisalign might do the job instead.

We'll talk through all of it at your free consultation.
