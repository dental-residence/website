Toothache · Same-day appointments

Toothache

Sudden, sharp or a dull ache that won’t go away? Call us in the morning and we’ll aim to see you that day.

Book Online Call (02) 8384 6667

Toothache has many causes. Some are minor; others are a dental emergency. If you have sudden, severe pain or an ache that keeps coming back, it is best to have it looked at soon. There is no need to put up with it.

Causes of Toothache

Toothache can come from tooth decay, a gum infection, a broken tooth or filling, clenching or grinding, a wisdom tooth coming through, a sinus infection or an abscess under the gum.

What to Expect On Your Visit

First We listen We ask about the pain: where it is, when it started and what sets it off.
Then We find the cause An examination and x-rays show us what is going on.
Finally We treat it Often the same day. If not, we make sure you are comfortable until your next visit.

Good questions

Toothache FAQs

Can you see me today?

We keep time each day for dental pain. Call us in the morning on (02) 8384 6667 and we’ll aim to see you that day.

What can I do until my appointment?

Rinse with warm salt water, take over-the-counter pain relief as directed on the pack, and avoid very hot, cold or sweet food on that side.

When is toothache an emergency?

Swelling in your face or jaw, a fever, a knocked-out tooth or pain you can’t control. Call us straight away; if you have trouble breathing or swallowing, go to hospital.
