Invisalign® at Dental Residence

Invisalign® Assessment

Straighter teeth, minus the metal.

Book Online Call (02) 8384 6667

Invisalign® uses a series of clear aligners that slip on, come out for dinner, and keep a very low profile in between. It suits adults and teens who’d like to sort out crowding, gaps or some bite issues, without the train-track look. It isn’t for everyone, though, so it all starts with an Invisalign Consultation with Dr Simon Khalil. Expect honest answers, no pressure, and the cost on the table before you decide a thing.

From first chat to final smile

How Invisalign Works

  1. ConsultAn Invisalign Consultation with Dr Simon Khalil to see if it suits you.
  2. ScanA digital scan of your teeth, no goopy impressions, to plan your treatment.
  3. WearClear aligners you swap as directed, taking them out to eat and brush.
  4. Check inRegular visits to make sure everything is moving as planned.

Good questions

Invisalign Assessment FAQs

Who will I see?

Your Invisalign Consultation is with Dr Simon Khalil.

Is Invisalign right for me?

It suits many adults and teens with crowding, gaps or some bite issues, but not everyone. Your consultation will give you an honest answer.

Can I take the aligners out?

Yes. You take them out to eat, drink anything other than water, and brush.

Can I spread the cost?

Yes: Afterpay, Zip, humm, or one of our in-house payment plans.
