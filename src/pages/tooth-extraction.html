Tooth removal · Petersham

Expert Tooth Extraction

When a tooth can’t be saved, we remove it carefully, explain every step and look after you afterwards.

Book Online Call (02) 8384 6667

We always try to save your natural teeth first. If a tooth does need to come out, we assess it with x-rays to plan the simplest, most comfortable way to remove it. Local anaesthetic is used for every extraction, and sedation is available if you are nervous. If you have a broken tooth or severe pain that can’t wait, we’ll aim to see you the same day.

What to Expect with a Tooth Extraction

Assess X-rays & a plan We examine the tooth, take x-rays and explain whether a simple or surgical extraction is needed.
Remove Gently done Under local anaesthetic, with sedation available, we remove the tooth as gently as possible.
Recover Home the same day You can usually go straight home, with clear aftercare instructions.

After your Tooth Extraction

Bite gently on the gauze we give you to stop the bleeding. If it starts again, bite down gently on a clean, folded tissue until it stops.

Avoid alcohol and smoking for at least 24 hours to help healing and prevent dry socket, and avoid strenuous activity, hot drinks and crunchy foods for about three days. Over-the-counter pain relief can help; avoid aspirin, which can increase bleeding.

Good questions

Tooth Extraction FAQs

Will it hurt?

The area is numbed with local anaesthetic, so you should feel pressure but not pain. Sedation is available if you are anxious.

How long does it take?

Allow about 45 minutes for the appointment. Simple extractions are often quicker.

Do you remove wisdom teeth?

Yes. See our wisdom tooth removal page for more.

What can I replace the tooth with?

Options include a dental implant, a bridge or dentures. We’ll talk you through them.
