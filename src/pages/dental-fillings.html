Fillings & tooth repair · Petersham

Dental Fillings

Tooth-coloured fillings that repair decay, chips and worn fillings, matched to your natural tooth.

Book Online Call (02) 8384 6667

Almost everyone needs a filling at some point. A filling seals a small hole, or cavity, in the enamel and protects the softer tooth underneath. A small filling now is a good investment: left alone, the hole usually grows, and so does the bill.

What to Expect On Your Visit

First A look and a plan We check the tooth, usually with an x-ray, and explain what it needs before we start.
Then Clean & repair We clean out the decay and prepare the tooth so the filling bonds well.
Finally Shaped to match The tooth-coloured material sets quickly, and we shape and polish it to feel like your own tooth.

Inlays and Onlays

When a hole is too large for a regular filling, an inlay or onlay may be the better choice. An inlay fills the channels between the cusps of the tooth, while an onlay covers one or more cusps. Both protect the tooth while keeping more of its natural structure.

If the top of the tooth is badly decayed but the base is still healthy, your dentist may recommend a crown. We can make same-day crowns with our CEREC machine, so you can often leave with it the same day.

Good questions

Dental Fillings FAQs

How long does a filling take?

Usually 30 to 45 minutes. Small fillings can sometimes be done during your regular check-up.

Will it hurt?

We use local anaesthetic, so most people feel little more than some pressure. If you are nervous, tell us when you book: happy gas is available at no extra cost.

What are the fillings made of?

Tooth-coloured composite, shaded to match your natural teeth.

Can I eat afterwards?

Yes. The material sets straight away, but if you have had anaesthetic, wait until the numbness wears off so you don’t bite your cheek.
