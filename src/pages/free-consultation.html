A Complimentary Consultation
with Dr Mac or Dr Anna

Introducing Dr Mac Dean and Dr Anna Yip

The Consultation

Dr Mac Dean and Dr Anna Yip are now consulting at Dental Residence. By way of introduction, they are offering a complimentary first consultation — at no cost, and with no obligation to proceed.

A Thorough Examination

A careful assessment of your teeth, gums and bite, including anything that has been on your mind.

An Honest Conversation

A chance to talk through what you would like to change, and any apprehension you may have about visiting the dentist.

A Detailed Treatment Plan

A considered plan of your own: what is recommended, what each stage involves, what it costs, and what is genuinely a priority.
This appointment covers examination and treatment planning. X-rays, cleans and any treatment are quoted separately at our standard rates — you will always know the cost before anything goes ahead.

Introducing Dr Mac and Dr Anna

Dr Mac Dean

Dr Mac Dean graduated from the University of Otago in New Zealand with a Bachelor of Dental Surgery. He is passionate about providing high-quality, patient-centred care, and believes every patient deserves to feel comfortable, informed and confident throughout their dental journey.

Dr Anna Yip

Dr Anna Yip is a general and cosmetic dentist known for her gentle approach and for the calm, comfortable environment she creates for patients of all ages. She has a particular interest in aesthetic dentistry, taking a meticulous approach to natural, balanced and refined results.

Arranging Your Visit

Book online or call the practice. Let our team know you would like to see Dr Mac or Dr Anna, and we will arrange the rest.
You will find us at 4/2 Albert Street, Marrickville, with entry on Addison Rd — a short drive from Petersham, Stanmore, Dulwich Hill, Enmore and Newtown. We accept all major Australian health funds with HICAPS on the spot, and offer interest-free payment plans and AfterPay for any treatment you decide to go ahead with.
Offer terms: The complimentary consultation is available for appointments with Dr Mac Dean or Dr Anna Yip only. It covers an examination, a discussion of your concerns and a detailed treatment plan. X-rays, cleans, and any dental treatment are not included and are charged at our standard rates — you will be given a written quote before any treatment begins. One complimentary consultation per person.
