Top 5 FAQs

Q1. Is teeth whitening safe for my teeth?
Yes, it has been clinically proven that teeth whitening does not damage your teeth.
Q2. Will it make my teeth sensitive?
Teeth whitening trays are a fast but gentle method of whitening your teeth so the risk of sensitivity is lower. Some people may experience short-term sensitivity which can be managed by using the trays less often while taking longer to achieve your desired results.
Q3. How long does it take to get my trays made?
To get your trays made, you will need to book in for a session with our dental professionals to get an impression of your teeth. You can then pick up your trays from our reception in 2-4 days.
Q4. What's the difference between professional whitening trays and over-the-counter trays?
Professional whitening trays are custom-made to fit to your teeth ensuring perfect contact between the whitening gels and every surface of your teeth, while protecting the gums. In addition, professional gel kits are stronger so act faster, have sensitivity protection and contain fluoride to mineralise your teeth.
Q5. What's the difference between in-chair whitening and teeth whitening trays?
In-chair teeth whitening can give you whiter smile in just one 30-min session. Whitening trays take longer to get to your desired whiteness but once you have bought the trays, you only need to buy the gel every time you want to top up your whiteness.
