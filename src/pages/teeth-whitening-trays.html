Good questions

Top 5 FAQs

Is teeth whitening safe for my teeth?

Yes, when it’s done under the guidance of a dental professional.

Will it make my teeth sensitive?

Some people feel short-term sensitivity. If you do, whiten less often. It just takes a little longer to reach your shade.

Which option should I choose?

Opalescence Go is quick and convenient: no impressions, no wait. Custom trays fit your teeth exactly and you can reuse them with gel refills. We’ll help you pick.

How long does it take to get custom trays made?

Book in for an impression of your teeth, then pick up your trays from reception in 2–4 days.

What’s the difference between professional whitening and over-the-counter products?

Professional Opalescence gels are stronger than what you can buy at the chemist and are only available through dental practices. Custom trays also fit your teeth exactly, so the gel reaches every surface while your gums stay protected.
