Dental Implants

A new tooth, rooted in place. No gaps, no glue, no taking it out at night.

An implant is a small titanium post that sits in your jawbone, topped with a porcelain crown. It looks and works a lot like a real tooth. Not everyone is a candidate, so it all starts with a free consultation: we’ll talk you through your options, the plan and how to pay.

What are dental implants and how much do they cost?

Two parts: a titanium screw in the jawbone and a porcelain crown on top. Looked after well, an implant is built to last.

The cost depends on your mouth and your plan, so we quote it at your consultation. You can spread it with an interest-free plan, Afterpay, Zip or humm.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Quite possibly. We scan your mouth and jaw to check an implant can go in properly, then talk through anything else that matters, like your health and lifestyle. The easiest way to find out is a free consultation.

Other ways to fill the gap

Cheaper Alternatives

Partial and full dentures. The most budget-friendly option: removable teeth that fill the gaps. More on dentures.

Dental bridges. A false tooth held in place by crowns on the teeth either side. Handy when there isn’t enough bone for an implant, though it means reshaping those neighbouring teeth. More on bridges.

Good questions

Dental Implants FAQs

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The complimentary consultation with Dr Mac or Dr Anna covers an examination, a chat about what you want and a treatment plan. X-rays, scans and any treatment are quoted separately before anything goes ahead.

How long does the whole process take?

It depends on your mouth. Implants usually take a few months from start to finish, because the bone needs time to heal around the post. We’ll give you a timeline at your consultation.

Does it hurt?

Implants are placed under local anaesthetic. If you are nervous, tell us: happy gas and oral sedation are available at no extra cost.

Can I spread the cost?

Yes. We offer interest-free payment plans for up to two years, plus Afterpay, Zip and humm.
