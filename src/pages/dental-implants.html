Dental Implants

Dental Implants and Alternative Solutions

Dental implants are currently the best solution for replacing missing teeth. However, there are other solutions starting from $2000.

In addition, not everyone is a candidate for dental implants. That is why we suggest that you talk to our specialist dentists who can help you make the right  decision for you. They can guide you through treatment plans as well as payment options.

What are dental implants and how much do they cost?

Dental implants are made up of two parts - a titanium screw implanted into the jaw bone and a porcelain crown that sits on top.

They look and act almost identically to a real tooth. As such, most dental implants are a life long investment if looked after properly.

Dental implants do come with a high price tag of between $3000-$6000 per tooth. However, interest free payment options are available to help reduce the impact on your back pocket.  

Cheaper Alternatives

But don't worry, if this is beyond your budget. There are cheaper alternatives such as partial dentures or dental bridges that can help give you back your smile.

Partial and Full Dentures

The cheapest alternative to dental implants are partial dentures. These start from $800 depending on the complexity of the denture.

The denture is a prosthetic tooth that can be slipped into and out of the mouth to fill in missing teeth. They look and act very similar to real teeth.

If very few healthy teeth remain, it may be necessary to go to a full denture, which involve removing the remaining teeth and providing you with a full set of artificial teeth.

Dental Bridges

A dental bridge involves attaching a false tooth to the teeth either side. This can be a good alternative when the bone density in the jaw is too low for an implant.

However, at around $4500 per tooth they are not much cheaper than an implant. In addition, they may require healthy teeth to be worn down in order to attach the bridge to the neighbouring teeth. Therefore, it is important to speak to a dentist to decide if this option is right for you.
