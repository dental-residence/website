What is a Crown

A crown is a porcelain cap for a tooth that’s badly broken or decayed. It looks and works like a natural tooth, and it sits on what’s left of yours, so we keep as much of your healthy tooth as we can.

Same day, start to finish

How Does the CEREC Machine Work?

  1. ScanA quick digital scan of your teeth. No impression trays.
  2. DesignWe design your crown on screen to fit your bite.
  3. MillCEREC carves it from a solid porcelain block while you wait.
  4. FitWe fit it at the same appointment.

Missing a tooth?

Dental Bridges

A bridge fills the gap with a false tooth, held in place by crowns on the teeth either side. Because it’s built from crowns, CEREC can make it in a couple of hours too.

Implants are the other permanent option, and we’ll help you weigh them up.

Good questions

Dental Crowns While You Wait FAQs

How long does a same-day crown take?

Usually a couple of hours, in a single visit.

Does it hurt?

We numb the tooth with local anaesthetic, so most people feel little more than some pressure. If you are nervous, let us know: happy gas is available at no extra cost.

Do I need a temporary crown?

No. Because we make the crown while you wait, there is no temporary crown and no second visit to fit it.

Can I claim on my health fund?

Depending on your level of cover, yes. We accept all major health funds with HICAPS, and nib members may pay less with us as a nib First Choice provider.
