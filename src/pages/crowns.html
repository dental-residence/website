Dental Crowns While You Wait

One visit. One crown. No goopy impressions, and no waiting weeks for a lab.

Our CEREC scanner and in-house mill make your crown while you wait, so a job that usually takes two visits a few weeks apart is done in one.

Not sure you need a crown? Book a Same-Day Crown Assessment and we'll take a look.

What is a Crown

A crown is a porcelain cap for a tooth that's badly broken or decayed. It looks and works like a natural tooth, and it sits on what's left of yours, so we keep as much of your healthy tooth as we can.

How Does the CEREC Machine Work?

  1. Scan. A quick digital scan of your teeth. No impression trays.
  2. Design. We design your crown on screen to fit your bite.
  3. Mill. CEREC carves it from a solid porcelain block while you wait.
  4. Fit. We fit it at the same appointment.

Dental Bridges

Missing a single tooth? A bridge fills the gap with a false tooth, held in place by crowns on the teeth either side. Because it's built from crowns, CEREC can make it in a couple of hours too.

Implants are the other permanent option, and we'll help you weigh them up. Read more about bridges.
