Check-ups & cleans · Petersham

Dental Checkup & Clean

A visit every six months is the simplest way to keep small problems small, and the dentist’s chair somewhere you sit by choice, not because something hurts.

Book Online Call (02) 8384 6667

In the same way you would service your car, your teeth need regular maintenance. A check-up and clean catches problems early, while they are simpler and less expensive to fix, and leaves your mouth feeling fresh and your breath clean.

What to Expect on Your Visit

The check-up A thorough look We check your teeth, gums and bite, and talk through anything that has been on your mind.
The clean Scale & polish We remove the build-up that brushing can’t reach, then polish your teeth smooth.
The plan X-rays & a clear plan On your first visit we take x-rays and show you what we see. Any treatment is quoted before we start.

Good questions

Check-up & Clean FAQs

How often should I have a check-up and clean?

For most people, every six months. Your dentist will let you know if you would benefit from coming in more or less often.

How long does it take?

About 45 to 60 minutes for your first visit, and 30 to 45 minutes after that.

Will it hurt?

Most people find a check-up and clean comfortable. If your teeth are sensitive or you are nervous, let us know when you book. We will go gently, and happy gas is available at no extra cost.

Can I claim on my health fund?

Yes. We accept all major health funds with HICAPS, so you can claim on the spot. We are also a nib First Choice provider, so nib members may pay less out of pocket.

Who will I see?

Dr Anna Yip, Dr Mac Dean or Rita Chen, our oral health therapist.
