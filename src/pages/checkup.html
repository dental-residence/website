Dental Checkup & Clean

The best way to avoid going to the dentist is to go to the dentist...crazy right? While we love to see our patients, we would prefer to see them every 6 months rather than for dental emergencies.

​In the same way that you need to maintain your car, you also need to maintain your mouth. That is the best way to avoid major, unexpected dental bills in the future.

A 6-monthly checkup and clean will not only ensure that your teeth stay healthy, but will have your mouth feeling fresh and your breath smelling great.  

What to Expect on Your Visit

Believe it or not a dental checkup and clean is more like a spa treatment than a medical procedure. It is pain free and that will leave you feeling fresh and rejuvenated. 

As part of every visit your dentist will provide you with a:
  • Comprehensive oral examination and consultation.
  • Full teeth scale and clean to leave your mouth feeling fresh.
  • Complete set of X-rays including a whole-mouth X-ray
  • Fluoride treatment for ongoing protection.

Based on the oral examination and the detailed X-rays, the dentist will let you know of any possible problem areas and advise you on any treatment that my be necessary to help keep your mouth in tip-top shape.

Your dental expert can also give you instructions on the best ways to maintain your teeth including brushing and flossing.
