Why Choose Us?

Our patients find going to the dentist is an enjoyable experience because we put our patients at the centre of everything that we do. From our staff to our technology and even our convenient suburban location, everything we do is focussing on giving you optimum comfort and the highest level of dental care. 
We invite you to try our unique, patient-centred care. We are confident that it will be the best dental experience you've ever had.
Dr Simon Khalil has put together a team of dental professionals who are caring and empathetic but also bring a bright, friendly and even fun atmosphere to the dentist. Our friendly team of expert dentists are accessible and always willing to help. 

The dental clinic is filled with all the latest technologies that ensure that your treatment is as quick and painless as possible and get you smiling again ASAP.  And, we are conveniently located within walking distance from Newtown and Marrickville and just short 15min drive from the CBD with free and easy street parking.

Caring for the Community
We believe our patients deserve to be treated with respect and compassion. We listen carefully to your concerns, goals and feedback throughout your visit so that you’ll be pleased with the results.
​
We promise timely, efficient service, quality dental materials and technology, and friendly and helpful staff. We’re passionate about what we do and we’ll take great care of you and your family!

Comfort and Convenience
​At the Residence, we do everything we can to make sure that your experience is as enjoyable as possible. When you walk through our doors, you are a Guest and an extended member of our Family.

We offer sedation dentistry to put you right at ease, or for something lighter, try our calming tea.

​We invite you into a beautiful, relaxed setting where we create the most pleasant, comfortable and positive experience for your visit with the Dentist.

Care and Commitment
We believe that the best way to avoid anxiety and discomfort during a dental procedure is to make sure that our Guests are well-informed and aware of every step of their procedure. 

​With technology and a human touch driving our day-to-day procedures, we empower you to to make decisions based on the goals we create together. 

Each member of our Family regularly participates in further education to ensure they are up to date on all of the latest technology and techniques. 

We strive to ensure that our Guests at the Residence have the best experience and result they have ever had at the Dentist!

Honesty and Affordability
We know that the cost of dental care can be confusing. In addition to offering a number of in-house payment options to fit your budget, we give you up-front cost information on your treatment ahead of time either emailed or printed. We are happy to help you navigate your dental health insurance and alternative payment options.
