Root canal treatment · Petersham

Root Canal Therapy

Treating the infection deep inside a tooth so you can keep it, rather than lose it.

Book Online Call (02) 8384 6667

Teeth are hard on the outside but alive inside, with nerves and blood vessels running down through the roots. When deep decay reaches them and they become infected, it can be very painful. Even if the pain settles, the infection needs treatment. Root canal therapy clears it from inside the tooth while keeping the tooth itself.

What to Expect on Your Visit

Step one Comfortable first We numb the area with local anaesthetic, with sedation available if you need it.
Step two Clean the canals We open the tooth, clean the infection out of the roots and make sure they are clear.
Step three Seal & protect The roots are filled and sealed, and the tooth is closed with a temporary filling while it settles.

After Root Canal Therapy

Once the tooth has settled, your dentist may recommend a crown to protect it, because a tooth that has had root canal treatment can be more brittle. We can often make that crown the same day with our CEREC machine.

Good questions

Root Canal Therapy FAQs

Does root canal treatment hurt?

It is done under local anaesthetic, so most people find it no more uncomfortable than a filling. Sedation is available if you are nervous.

How many visits will I need?

Often two or more, depending on the tooth and the infection. Your dentist will explain the plan at your consultation.

Will I need a crown afterwards?

Often, yes, to protect the tooth. We can usually make it the same day with our CEREC machine.

What if I don’t treat it?

The infection can spread and the tooth may eventually need to come out. It is worth having it checked early.
