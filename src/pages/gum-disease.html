Gum Disease

Gum Disease

Gum disease can be treated easily and effectively if caught early. However, if left for too long, gum disease can cause serious problems in your mouth as well as your gut. That is why we recommend 6-monthly visits to stay on top of any issues.

Symptoms of Gum Disease

If you're experiencing any of the following symptoms then it is important that you get your gums checked out by one of our dental professionals at the dental residence. Usually the solution is very simple and painless and so there is no reason to stay in discomfort.
  • Persistent bad breath (Halitosis)
  • Red and inflamed gums
  • Tender gums
  • bleeding gums
  • Painful chewing
  • Loose teeth
  • Sensitive teeth
  • Receding gums

How We Treat Gum Disease

The main goal to solve gum disease is to get rid of the infection that is caused by bacteria buildup in the mouth. 

On your first visit, your dental professional will perform a painless oral examination and usually take X-rays to assess the extent of the damage and determine if there are any additional problems that could affect treatment.

You dentist may perform a deep clean using our advanced LED laser technology to clear away the infection. You may be prescribe antibiotics or an antibiotic mouthwash to help treat the infection.

Once the infection has cleared, you may also require dental fissures which are like filling for the gums to help keep out bacteria from the gums.

In extreme cases, you may need to be referred to a Periodontists, who specialises in this specific problem.
