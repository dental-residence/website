Gum health · Petersham

Gum Disease

Bleeding, tender or receding gums are worth checking early, when gum disease is simplest to treat.

Book Online Call (02) 8384 6667

Caught early, gum disease can be treated simply and effectively. Left too long, it can lead to loose teeth and affect your general health. That is why we recommend a check-up and clean every six months.

Symptoms of Gum Disease

If you notice any of these, it is worth having your gums checked:

  • Persistent bad breath
  • Red, swollen or tender gums
  • Bleeding gums
  • Painful chewing
  • Loose or sensitive teeth
  • Receding gums

How We Treat Gum Disease

Assess Exam & x-rays We check your gums gently and usually take x-rays to see how far it has gone.
Treat A deep clean We clear away the bacteria and build-up that cause the infection, below the gum line.
Maintain Keep it healthy We show you how to keep your gums healthy at home, with regular cleans to stay on top of it.

Good questions

Gum Disease FAQs

Why do my gums bleed when I brush?

Bleeding is often an early sign of gum inflammation. It is worth having it checked rather than brushing less.

How long is a deep clean?

Allow about 60 minutes.
