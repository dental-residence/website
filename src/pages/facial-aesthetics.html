Good questions

Facial Aesthetics FAQs

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes. Every treatment begins with a consultation and an individual assessment with Dr Simon Khalil. Not everyone is suitable for every treatment, and we will tell you honestly if something is not right for you.

What happens at the consultation?

We listen to what you notice and what you would like to change. Dr Simon then assesses your face, expression and medical history, and gives you a written plan and quote, with time to think it over. After any treatment, we see you again to check you are happy with how things have settled.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on your assessment and the plan we agree on. You will receive a written quote at your consultation, before any treatment.

Why a dentist?

Dentists work with the muscles of the face and jaw every day. For concerns like jaw clenching, teeth grinding or a gummy smile, that understanding is especially relevant.

Are there risks?

As with any medical treatment, there are risks and possible side effects. Dr Simon will explain these at your consultation so you can make an informed decision.
