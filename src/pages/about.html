About Dental Residence

Dentistry, considered differently.

At Dental Residence, we believe dental care should feel personal, comfortable and empowering.
Our practice is built on genuine relationships, clinical excellence and a calm, welcoming environment in the heart of Sydney’s Inner West.
We take the time to understand you, listen to your goals and create a treatment plan that’s right for you.

Our Dental Services

We offer comprehensive dental care using modern technology and a gentle, personalised approach.
From everyday care to advanced treatment, we’re here to help you achieve and maintain a healthy, confident smile.

IN THE HEART OF THE INNER WEST

A beautiful location. Easy to visit.

Tucked away on a quiet, leafy street in Petersham, Dental Residence is just moments from Newtown and Marrickville.
Set within a character-filled Federation cottage, our practice was created to feel calm, warm and welcoming — a world away from the traditional clinical environment.
With convenient access and free nearby parking, visiting the dentist feels that little bit easier.

Your Comfort, Our Priority

From the moment you arrive, our friendly team are here to make you feel at ease.
We listen, we explain, and we make sure you feel informed and comfortable throughout your care.

MODERN DENTISTRY, THOUGHTFULLY DELIVERED

Advanced care. A better experience.

Behind our relaxed surroundings is a practice equipped with advanced dental technology, helping us provide precise, efficient and comfortable care.

With our CEREC technology, we can digitally scan, design and create custom ceramic crowns and veneers right here at Dental Residence, often in a single appointment.
That means no traditional impressions, no temporary crown and, in many cases, no waiting weeks for your restoration to return from a dental laboratory.
​
One visit. One beautifully made restoration.

Same-Day Appointments

We understand that dental problems don’t always happen at a convenient time.
Where availability allows, we offer same-day appointments for patients who need to be seen quickly — whether you’re experiencing pain, have broken a tooth, or simply need some reassurance.
Book online at a time that suits you, or call our team and we’ll do our best to accommodate you as soon as possible.

Personalised Payment Options

Quality dental care should feel straightforward — including when it comes to payment.
We accept all major Australian health funds, with HICAPS available for convenient on-the-spot claims.
For eligible treatments, flexible payment options are also available, allowing you to spread the cost of your care over time.
Our team is always happy to talk you through the options available before treatment, so you know exactly what to expect.
