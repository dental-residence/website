In pain? Start here

Dental Emergency? We’ll See You Today

A dental emergency rarely waits for a convenient time. Severe toothache, a knocked-out tooth or a broken crown needs fast attention — both to relieve the pain and to give your tooth the best chance of being saved. We keep time set aside every day for same-day emergency appointments at our practice on the Petersham–Marrickville border, minutes from Stanmore, Dulwich Hill, Enmore and Newtown.

Call us on (02) 8384 6667 and our team will prioritise you — in most cases we can see you the same day.

What we see

Emergencies we treat

  • Severe toothache and dental pain
  • Knocked-out (avulsed) teeth
  • Chipped, cracked or broken teeth
  • Lost fillings and crowns
  • Dental abscess and facial swelling
  • Bleeding after an extraction
  • Broken dentures
  • Sports injuries to teeth and gums

Before you get here

What to do right now

  • Knocked-out tooth: handle it by the crown (not the root), rinse briefly in milk or saline, and if you can, place it gently back in the socket — otherwise keep it in milk. The first 30–60 minutes matter most.
  • Severe toothache: rinse with warm salt water and take your usual pain relief. Never place aspirin directly on the gum.
  • Facial or jaw swelling: call us immediately — swelling can become serious quickly. If you have difficulty breathing or swallowing, go straight to the nearest hospital emergency department.
  • Broken tooth or lost crown: keep any pieces, avoid chewing on that side, and cover sharp edges with sugar-free chewing gum or dental wax.

Good questions

Emergency dental FAQs

How quickly can I be seen in a dental emergency?

We reserve time each day for dental emergencies and will always try to see you the same day you call. Phone (02) 8384 6667 as early in the day as you can.

I’m not an existing patient — can I still come in?

Yes. New patients with dental emergencies are always welcome — many of our long-term patients first came to us in an emergency.

What if my emergency happens outside opening hours?

Call (02) 8384 6667 and follow the instructions on our message. For severe swelling, uncontrolled bleeding or trauma, go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
