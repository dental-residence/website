Dental Emergency? We’ll See You Today

A dental emergency rarely waits for a convenient time. Severe toothache, a knocked-out tooth or a broken crown needs fast attention — both to relieve the pain and to give your tooth the best chance of being saved. We keep time set aside every day for same-day emergency appointments at our Marrickville practice, minutes from Petersham, Stanmore, Dulwich Hill, Enmore and Newtown.

Call us on (02) 8384 6667 and our team will prioritise you — in most cases we can see you the same day.

Emergencies we treat

• Severe toothache and dental pain
• Knocked-out (avulsed) teeth
• Chipped, cracked or broken teeth
• Lost fillings and crowns
• Dental abscess and facial swelling
• Bleeding after an extraction
• Broken dentures
• Sports injuries to teeth and gums

What to do right now

Knocked-out tooth: handle it by the crown (not the root), rinse briefly in milk or saline, and if you can, place it gently back in the socket — otherwise keep it in milk. The first 30–60 minutes matter most.

Severe toothache: rinse with warm salt water and take your usual pain relief. Never place aspirin directly on the gum.

Facial or jaw swelling: call us immediately — swelling can become serious quickly. If you have difficulty breathing or swallowing, go straight to the nearest hospital emergency department.

Broken tooth or lost crown: keep any pieces, avoid chewing on that side, and cover sharp edges with sugar-free chewing gum or dental wax.
