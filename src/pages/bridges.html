Dental bridges · Petersham

Dental Bridges

A fixed replacement for a missing tooth, often made in-house the same day with our CEREC machine.

Book Online Call (02) 8384 6667

A dental bridge permanently replaces a single missing tooth when the teeth either side are reasonably healthy. Crowns are placed on those neighbouring teeth, holding an artificial tooth between them, so it “bridges” the gap. With our CEREC machine, a process that usually takes weeks can often be done in a couple of hours.

Know your options

Alternatives

The other permanent option is a dental implant. Implants cost more up front but often last longer, and they don’t need the neighbouring teeth to be prepared for crowns. A bridge can be the better choice where there is less bone in the jaw.

The right answer depends on you, which is why we offer a complimentary 30-minute consultation to talk it through.

Good questions

Dental Bridges FAQs

How long does a bridge take?

With our CEREC machine, a bridge can often be made and fitted in a couple of hours.

Bridge or implant?

Both are good options. We’ll explain the pros and cons for your situation at a complimentary consultation.

How do I look after a bridge?

Brush and floss as usual, including under the bridge, and keep up your regular check-ups.
