Dental Bridges

Dental bridges provide a permanent tooth replacement for a single missing tooth with relatively healthy teeth either side.

A dental bridge works by attaching crowns to the teeth either side of the missing tooth with an artificial tooth creating a "bridge" between. 

​And with our advanced CEREC machine, we are able to cut a process that would usually take weeks into just a couple of hours!

While dental bridges are a good option for many people, they may not be the best option for you. It is important that you have all of the right information so that you can make the right decision. That is why we offer free 30 min consultations with one of our dental professionals for people who would like to learn more about options for replacing missing teeth.
​

Alternatives

The other permanent tooth replacement solution is dental implants. While dental implants are slightly more expensive in the short term, they end up being better value for money in the long term since they tend to be more durable than a bridge.

In addition, dental implants do not require the healthy, neighbouring teeth to be worn down as is required for dental bridges.

However, dental bridges may be a superior solution in cases where the bone density in the jaw is low. Therefore, it is import to talk to one of our dental professionals who can help you identify the best treatment option for you.
